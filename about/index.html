About the firm

Prepared counsel from the heart of Nairobi.

Mukunga, Wathome & Co. Advocates is based at Phoenix House on Kenyatta Avenue and provides litigation-led legal support alongside advisory, employment, public-law and formal-document services.

A direct legal relationship built on preparation and clarity.

The firm's public court record includes Employment and Labour Relations Court proceedings and constitutional or public-law matters. The practice is positioned to support both individuals and organisations where a legal issue requires advice, negotiation or formal representation.

The firm is led by co-founders Mukunga and Edwin Dano.
